# Veda Technology Business & Service Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Data
Science solution for analyzing simulated Veda Technology business
activities.

The analysis covers:

- Website visitors
- Marketing channels
- Internship applications
- Training programs
- IT services
- Digital services
- Customer service requests
- Customer segmentation
- Service demand
- Revenue
- Customer satisfaction
- Predictive analytics

> **Data Disclaimer:** The dataset used in this project is completely
> synthetic and was created for analytical and educational purposes.
> No private or confidential Veda Technology data was used.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives are:

1. Understand website visitor behavior.
2. Analyze marketing-channel performance.
3. Analyze internship applications.
4. Evaluate training program performance.
5. Identify IT and digital service demand.
6. Analyze customer service requests.
7. Segment customers based on behavioral characteristics.
8. Identify important business KPIs.
9. Develop predictive analytics.
10. Provide data-driven business recommendations.

🤖 Machine Learning

Customer segmentation was performed using K-Means clustering.

Predictive modelling was performed using Random Forest Regression
to analyze revenue-related patterns.

Model performance was evaluated using appropriate regression metrics.

💡 Business Recommendations

Recommendations are generated from the observed patterns in the
synthetic dataset.

Examples include:

Focus marketing resources on channels showing stronger engagement
and conversion.
Prioritize operational capacity for high-demand services.
Improve response times for high-priority service requests.
Review training programs with lower completion or performance.
Develop differentiated engagement strategies for customer segments.
⚠️ Limitations

The dataset used in this project is synthetic.

Therefore, the results should not be interpreted as actual Veda
Technology business performance.

Real-world deployment would require validated operational data,
appropriate governance, privacy controls and continuous monitoring.
